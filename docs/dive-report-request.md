# Draft: note to Just Get Wet about their dive reports

To: Just Get Wet (contact form or email on justgetwet.com)
Subject: Using your San Diego dive reports to check a snorkel-conditions tool

Hi Just Get Wet team,

I'm Jay. I've built a small, non-commercial page that gives my partner a
daily "snorkel today?" call for La Jolla and a few other San Diego spots,
from public data: CDIP wave models, the Scripps Pier water sensors, NWS
forecasts and the county's water-quality advisories
(https://wujin31.github.io/helen-snorkels/).

Its weak point is visibility. No public sensor measures it, so the page
estimates it from swell, plankton and rain, and those estimates have never
been checked against what divers actually saw. Your dive reports are the best
record of that I've found.

Would you be OK with the project reading your dive-report posts (the blog at
/blogs/dive-reports-and-conditions, back to when they started) to pull out
the date, site and visibility, and using those numbers only to tune and score
its estimates? It would:

- read each post once, slowly, identifying itself in the User-Agent;
- keep only date, site, visibility and swell numbers, linking back to the
  post, never republishing your text or photos;
- credit Just Get Wet on the page wherever the accuracy record appears.

Your store currently answers automated requests with "429 Too Many
Requests", so the project hasn't read any reports, and won't without your
OK. If there's a feed or an export you'd rather I use, that's perfect too.

Thanks for the reports either way,
Jay
