# Draft: request to the Shore Stations team about the underwater cam

To: sioshorestation@gmail.com
Subject: Scripps Pier underwater cam: still-frame access for a small visibility project

Hi Shore Stations team,

I'm Jay, and I work with SIO through SPF. On the side I'm building a small,
non-commercial tool that gives my partner a daily "snorkel today?" call for La
Jolla Cove and La Jolla Shores. The piling guide on the PierViz page (4, 11,
14 and 30 ft) is the best visibility measurement around, so the tool reads the
underwater cam the way she already does by eye.

Right now it saves one still frame every 15 minutes in daylight from the public
stream, stored privately and not republished. I wanted to check that you're
comfortable with this, and ask:

1. Is there a preferred way to grab stills (a snapshot URL, an archive, or a
   rate you'd like us to stay under)?
2. Would you be OK with the latest frame appearing on the tool's page, credited
   to the Shore Stations Program, with the pilings outlined?
3. Would a record of cam-derived visibility be useful to you? I'm happy to
   share it and the code, and to flag outages or lens fouling when the frames
   show them.

I can show you a prototype whenever it suits. If you'd rather we didn't use the
stream this way, I'll stop capture right away.

Thanks,
Jay
